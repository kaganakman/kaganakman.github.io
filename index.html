Kağan Akman

Ph.D. Student at Bilkent University, Department of Mathematics

Research Interests

  • Stochastic Control Theory: Optimal control, robustness to incorrect models, finite model approximations, measure-valued Markov decision processes, reinforcement learning, optimal filtering.
  • Machine Learning: Mathematical foundations of deep learning, control--theoretic approaches to Transformers, generalization problem, expressivity of neural networks.
  • Game Theory: Mean-field games, graphon games, information-structural properties of equilibria, existence of Nash equilibria in discounted games.
  • Optimal Transport: Gradient flows on the space of probability measures, optimal transport formulations of statistical learning.
